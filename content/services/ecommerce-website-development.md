## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Should I choose Shopify or WooCommerce for my e-commerce business?

It depends on your business model: choose Shopify if you want a reliable hosted solution where you never have to manage servers, security patches, or checkout scaling yourself, and are comfortable paying monthly fees. Choose WooCommerce if you want 100% data ownership, zero platform transaction percentages, complete control over customer data, and deep custom business logic or Indian payment flow overrides.

### Q2: How do you fix high mobile cart abandonment for Indian shoppers?

We eliminate the top four reasons Indian shoppers drop off: 1) we remove mandatory password creation, allowing pure 10-digit mobile number guest checkout; 2) we auto-fill City and State the moment 6 digits of Pincode are entered; 3) we implement deep UPI Intent (PhonePe/GPay app-switching) instead of showing static desktop QR codes; and 4) we integrate WebOTP autofill so SMS verification takes zero manual typing.

### Q3: Can your stores handle sudden flash sales or high festival traffic?

Yes. For WooCommerce stores, we configure Redis in-memory object caching, OPcache PHP acceleration, and high-concurrency database connection pooling. Static assets are served via Cloudflare global edge network. For Shopify, custom Liquid themes are architected to operate well within Shopify’s API rate limits, ensuring sub-second performance even during Diwali or Black Friday traffic surges.

### Q4: How do you handle Indian GST invoice generation and HSN compliance?

We configure automated GST calculation (CGST + SGST for intra-state, IGST for inter-state) based on customer delivery pincodes. For B2B clients, we include an optional B2B GSTIN field at checkout that validates the corporate GST number in real time and automatically prints a compliant GST tax invoice with HSN codes upon purchase.

### Q5: Can you integrate our existing warehouse ERP or physical retail POS?

Yes. We have built custom bi-directional webhooks and API sync pipelines connecting online store inventory with SAP, Zoho Inventory, Busy, Tally, and custom ERP systems. When an item is purchased in an offline retail store, stock counts update online within seconds, preventing accidental overselling.

### Q6: What is your typical build timeline for a custom e-commerce store?

A full custom storefront—including bespoke design, payment gateway integration, shipping automation, catalog import, and mobile testing—typically takes 3 to 5 weeks. We provide clear milestone reviews at every stage so your team can upload products while engineering is underway.
