## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Will our website lose its current Google search rankings during or after the redesign?

No. When executed correctly, a redesign should actually improve your search rankings due to faster Core Web Vitals and better mobile UX. Before changing any code, we extract 100% of your domain's historical URLs, indexed backlinks, and metadata using headless crawlers. We configure strict 1-to-1 301 redirection maps at the server level, preserve existing URL slugs wherever possible, carry over all schema markup, and monitor Google Search Console indexing continuously post-launch.

### Q2: How do you guarantee that our site will achieve sub-0.8s mobile load times?

We approach speed as a multi-tier engineering discipline rather than just installing a caching plugin. We de-bloat frontend assets by stripping unused CSS and deferring non-critical JavaScript, compress and convert all images into next-gen WebP/AVIF formats, optimize backend database queries and transient options in MySQL, and deploy server-level Redis caching alongside Cloudflare HTTP/3 edge routing. This guarantees green 90+ Core Web Vitals scores.

### Q3: Is our existing website taken offline while the redesign is being built?

Not at all. Your live website remains 100% operational, accepting customer orders and lead inquiries throughout the entire project. The new design and speed optimizations are engineered on an isolated, password-protected staging server. Once you review and approve the final version, we perform the live DNS switchover during off-peak hours with zero downtime.

### Q4: Can we keep our existing CMS and database while only redesigning the frontend?

Yes. If your backend CMS (e.g. WordPress, custom admin) works well for your administrative workflow, we can perform a surgical frontend architecture rebuild. We retain your database, content, and posts intact while replacing the sluggish presentation layer with a modern, ultra-fast custom theme or decoupled headless frontend.

### Q5: What causes most corporate websites to become slow and bloated over time?

The primary culprits are commercial page builders (Elementor, Divi), excessive third-party plugins (each loading their own CSS and JS on every page), uncompressed high-resolution images uploaded by staff, orphaned database transients, and cheap shared hosting without opcode or Redis caching. We systematically remediate each of these bottlenecks.

### Q6: Do you configure Google Search Console and verify indexing post-launch?

Yes. Post-launch verification is included in every project. We resubmit your new XML sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, monitor the Coverage and Indexation tabs for 404 crawl errors, test real-user Core Web Vitals field data, and provide a 30-day post-launch warranty to guarantee stability.
