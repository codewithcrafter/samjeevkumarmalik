## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can our marketing team edit text, images, and pages without writing code?

Yes, 100%. That is the primary advantage of custom Gutenberg block architecture. We build modular, intuitive visual blocks tailored to your exact design system. Your marketing team can add sections, edit copy, update images, and publish new blog posts with complete drag-and-drop ease, without ever needing a developer or touching a line of code.

### Q2: Why do you avoid Elementor, Divi, and pre-made ThemeForest themes?

Commercial page builders inject dozens of nested div tags, unoptimized JavaScript libraries, and hundreds of kilobytes of unused CSS on every single page load. This causes severe mobile latency, lowers your Google Ads Quality Scores, and makes your site crash whenever WordPress releases an update. By writing custom PHP and native Gutenberg blocks, we achieve sub-0.8s load times and 100% platform stability.

### Q3: Will our existing Google SEO rankings be preserved during a redesign?

Yes. Protecting your search equity is our highest priority. Before writing any code, we conduct a complete URL crawl inventory of all indexed pages. We implement strict 1-to-1 301 redirection mapping, preserve high-ranking URL slug structures, carry over all meta tags and schema markup, and verify Google Search Console indexing immediately post-launch to ensure zero organic traffic loss.

### Q4: How do you secure WordPress without slowing it down with security plugins?

Most commercial security plugins perform heavy real-time PHP scans on your server, exhausting CPU resources. We implement security at the network edge and server layer: disabling XML-RPC, obfuscating login routes and database prefixes, enforcing two-factor authentication, configuring Cloudflare WAF firewall rules, and eliminating third-party plugin vulnerabilities entirely.

### Q5: Do we own the website code, designs, and intellectual property?

Absolutely. Unlike traditional agencies that hold clients hostage with proprietary platforms or monthly software rental fees, you receive 100% intellectual property ownership upon final milestone completion. We transfer the complete Git repository, database dumps, and full administrative credentials directly to your organization.

### Q6: What is your typical turnaround timeline and milestone structure?

A standard custom enterprise WordPress build takes 3 to 4 weeks from initial discovery to production launch. We operate on transparent milestone sprints: 40% upon architecture and wireframe sign-off, 30% upon staging environment component review, and 30% upon final production launch, performance audit verification, and Git handover.
