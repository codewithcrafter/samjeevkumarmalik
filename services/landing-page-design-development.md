## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Why shouldn't I just send Google Ads or Meta traffic to my existing homepage?

Homepages are designed for exploration, not direct conversion. They contain multiple navigation menus, links to company history, social media feeds, and disparate service categories that give visitors too many ways to leave without converting. A dedicated landing page strips away all distractions and focuses 100% of the visitor's attention on a single, compelling offer, typically multiplying conversion rates by 3x to 5x.

### Q2: How quickly can you design, code, and deploy a custom landing page?

Our standard landing page engineering sprint takes 5 to 7 business days from brief to live deployment. This includes offer framing, high-fidelity responsive wireframing, lightweight custom frontend coding, webhook plumbing for WhatsApp and CRM, and end-to-end pixel tracking verification.

### Q3: Do you integrate the landing page with our CRM and WhatsApp?

Yes, absolutely. We build direct API webhooks connecting form submissions to your CRM of choice (Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, LeadSquared) as well as direct WhatsApp Business API alerts to notify your sales reps within 30 seconds of lead capture.

### Q4: Will this landing page work on our existing domain or subdomain?

Yes. We can deploy the landing page seamlessly on a subfolder of your existing website (e.g. yourdomain.com/special-offer) or on a dedicated marketing subdomain (e.g. go.yourdomain.com), configured with custom SSL certificates and Cloudflare edge delivery.

### Q5: How does a fast landing page reduce our Google Ads Cost Per Click (CPC)?

Google's ad ranking algorithm relies heavily on Quality Score, which directly evaluates landing page experience, mobile page load speed, and content relevance. When your landing page loads in under 0.5 seconds with zero layout shift and tightly matched keywords, Google awards you a 9/10 or 10/10 Quality Score, which discounts your actual CPC bid by up to 30% while securing top ad positions.

### Q6: Do I have to pay ongoing monthly software fees like Unbounce or Leadpages?

No. You pay a one-time development fee for the engineering sprint. You own 100% of the code, designs, and assets outright. There are zero recurring platform subscription charges, zero traffic limits, and zero proprietary lock-in.
