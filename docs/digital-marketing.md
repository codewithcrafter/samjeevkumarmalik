## 1. Technical SEO & Performance Marketing Synergy

Most marketing teams spend heavily on Google Ads, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn campaigns while sending paid clicks to slow, generic websites that convert at less than 1.5%.

Sanjeev Kumar Malik bridges the gap between software engineering and performance marketing:

### Google Ads Quality Score Maximization
- **Load Time Under 0.6s:** Eliminates bounce rates from mobile paid search traffic.
- **Message Match Hierarchy:** High keyword relevance between ad copy, H1 headlines, and value propositions boosts Google Ads Quality Scores, immediately lowering Cost Per Click (CPC) by 20% to 40%.
- **Zero Layout Shifts:** CLS < 0.01 ensures users never tap the wrong element or abandon forms during load.

### Technical SEO & Organic Visibility
- **Schema.org Structured Data:** Complete JSON-LD implementation (Organization, ProfessionalService, WebSite, Service, BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList) for rich search snippets.
- **Core Web Vitals Compliance:** Passed CWV thresholds provide direct Google mobile search ranking preference.
- **Clean Semantic Markup:** Native HTML5 semantic tags, hierarchical headings (H1-H4), and optimized open graph metadata for viral social sharing.
