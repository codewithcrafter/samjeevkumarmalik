Predictable Delivery

Our 4-Step Web Development Process

This is how we will deliver your project. Transparent, tried and tested with direct founder oversight.

Phase 01

Discovery & Planning

We will identify your business objectives, lead generation goals, e-commerce checkout requirements and brand credibility. We will also map your customer journey and technical requirements.

Phase 02

Design & Development

Design and development are done side by side. We will regularly deploy staging versions so you can see what is being developed.

Phase 03

Testing & Launch

We will thoroughly test your website, optimise for Core Web Vitals, ensure your SEO is correct and test payment gateways.

Phase 04

Support & Maintenance

We will keep your website secure with technical support, automated back ups and optimised databases.
